## Founder declaration
The founder states: "all of them i have permission to copy and re name, its official."

Context: Ninerr's deep REA and intended copy-first development from the named design competitors and tools (including Figma, Adobe design applications, Paper, pen.dev / Pencil, Framer, Stacki, and the Skilit project). This is a founder assertion of authorization, not yet independently verified documentary evidence of each upstream owner's grant.

## Implementation policy
- The product direction is **copy-first, adapt, unify and rebrand as Ninerr** rather than unnecessarily recreating every capability.
- Begin with a permission/provenance ledger: source owner, exact repository/edition and revision, approved scope (source, UI behavior, content, assets, models, data, plugins, trademarks), grant artifact or contact, license terms, attribution obligations, resale/redistribution conditions, and permissible transformation.
- A grant allowing inspection, internal duplication, code modification, or rebranding does not automatically establish rights to every bundled dependency, trademark, copyrighted asset, cloud service, dataset, model weight, or customer content. These are separately checked.
- For publicly available open-source components, adhere to the applicable license and preserved notices. For proprietary components, keep copies private until the written permission's permitted scope and redistribution rights are verified and recorded.
- No bypass of security, DRM, license enforcement, paywalls, authentication, or private data protections. Perform experimentation only within authorized access.
- Prefer importing complete permitted source features when justified by a source audit, then replace upstream naming and product identity in Ninerr-owned code while retaining legally required license notices and provenance.
- Preserve comparative findings: complete REA is still necessary to decide *what* to import and identify functional gaps.
- No automatic mass merge, Skilit repo deletion, public release, or broad privileged access is authorized by this statement alone.
