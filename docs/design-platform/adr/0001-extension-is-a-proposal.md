# ADR-0001: The design-platform extension is a proposal after P10

- **Status:** proposed, 2026-10-18.
- **Context:** the founder authorized N0, P08, P09, P10 and the release gate (`docs/MASTER_PLAN.md`). The design-platform study proposes further phases.
- **Decision:**
  - The new phases are numbered P11 to P16, and **none starts before the founder approves it**.
  - P09 and P10 criteria, and the release gate, are unchanged. The proposal claims no approval.
  - The founder may approve the proposal as a whole or phase by phase. The first approvable unit is P11, task T11.1.
- **Consequences:**
  - These documents sit in `docs/design-platform/`. `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md` only points to them, as a proposal.
