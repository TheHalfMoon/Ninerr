## Limitations

- **Figma:** a Starter plan, so modes could not be tested; the remote MCP server itself was not connected; the built-in agent was given one request.
- **Claude Design:** the canvas editor, chat refinement and the product's own generation were not observed (E-CD-13).
- **Paper Desktop:** its window could not be observed with REA on Windows; its version is not reported.
- **Performance:** no competitor figure was measured, so the study makes no performance comparison.
- **Retrieval summaries:** documentation not read in full is marked medium or low, and is re-read before any implementation detail depends on it.
