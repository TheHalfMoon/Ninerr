## What changed in the journeys
- **B:** reorder with Alt+Up, move into and out of a layer with Alt+Right and Alt+Left, drag past a sibling in the layers tree, group with Ctrl+G, and set classes in the inspector. After each, one Undo must restore the whole document. Grouping must leave the box where the canvas drew it.
- **E:** the agent's change is offered as Undo agent change, named, beside Undo. Undoing it must restore the exact text from before the agent's edit, and Undo brings the edit back.

An earlier version of these checks ran at `e4e201f`. A fresh-context review found three could pass while the behaviour was false, so they were tightened and every project was run again. The results below replace that run.

## What this does and does not show for P09
- **Criterion 1 (agent undo):** exercised on all seven.
- **Criterion 2 (reorder, group, reparent):** Alt+Up, Alt+Right, Alt+Left, drag and Ctrl+G ran on all seven. They act on boxes the driver inserted into a project's page, not on the project's own layers.
- **Criterion 3 (classes):** on all seven, on an inserted box and on one of the project's own layers, keeping its existing classes.
- **Not covered here:**
  - reparenting by pointer, which `tests/layer-structure.test.mjs` covers on a fixture (this driver's drag only reorders);
  - Group by mouse, and a multi-layer group;
  - touch or pen;
  - writing a class back to source.

  Keyboard-only multi-select is tested in `tests/editor-accessibility.test.mjs`.
